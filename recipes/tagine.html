Chicken Tagine

Chicken slow-cooked with preserved lemons and olives in a conical clay pot.

About this dish

The tagine is both the name of the conical clay pot and the fragrant stew cooked inside it. Moroccan families have slow-cooked meat, vegetables and dried fruit this way for centuries. The cone traps steam so the food stays tender, and the spice blend of saffron, ginger and cumin gives it its unmistakable aroma.

🌍 Morocco⏱ Prep 20 min · Cook 1 h 15 min

Where it comes from

Ingredients

Servings: 4
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 150 g green olives
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250 ml chicken stock
  • 1 bunch fresh coriander

Cooking instructions

Show / hide the steps
  1. Mix the ginger, cumin, turmeric, saffron, garlic and oil, then rub the paste all over the chicken. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Scatter the onions in the base of the tagine (or a heavy casserole) and lay the chicken on top.
  3. Pour in the stock, cover, and cook over low heat for 50 minutes.
  4. Add the sliced preserved lemon rind and olives, then cook uncovered for 15 minutes to thicken the sauce.
  5. Finish with chopped coriander and serve with warm bread or couscous.
