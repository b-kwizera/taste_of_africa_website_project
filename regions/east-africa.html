East Africa

From Ethiopian highland stews to Swahili coastal curries and Rwandan leaf dishes, East Africa is full of variety.

Food culture

The Ethiopian table revolves around injera, shared from one large platter and eaten by hand. On the Swahili coast, trade with Arabia and India brought cardamom, clove and coconut into the kitchen, while Rwandan and Ugandan cooks simmer leaves and beans in groundnut sauces.

Staple ingredients

  • Teff, maize (ugali) and sorghum
  • Beans, lentils and leafy greens
  • Coconut, plantain and cassava
  • Berbere, cardamom, clove and cinnamon

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting teff batter for injera
  • Slow-simmering onions for wat stews
  • Cooking with coconut milk on the coast
  • Pounding and boiling cassava leaves
