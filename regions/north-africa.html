North Africa

Aromatic and gently sweet, North African cuisine blends Berber, Arab and Mediterranean traditions.

Food culture

Cooking in the Maghreb is slow and fragrant. Families gather around steaming platters of couscous on Fridays, and spice markets sell blends such as ras el hanout made from dozens of spices. Sweet and savoury flavours meet in dishes with dried fruit, olives and preserved lemon.

Staple ingredients

  • Couscous and semolina
  • Olives, dates and preserved lemons
  • Lamb, chicken and chickpeas
  • Cumin, saffron, cinnamon and harissa

Cooking techniques

  • Slow-cooking in a clay tagine
  • Steaming couscous in a couscoussier
  • Preserving lemons in salt
  • Baking bread in communal wood ovens
