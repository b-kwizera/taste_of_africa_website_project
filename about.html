About Taste of Africa

Honouring the continent's food heritage, one recipe at a time.

Our mission

To promote, preserve and celebrate African food heritage by making its recipes, ingredients and stories easy to explore for everyone.

A brief history of African cuisine

  • Ancient times: Grains such as sorghum, millet and teff are among the first crops domesticated on the continent.
  • Trade routes: Saharan caravans and Indian Ocean traders carry spices, rice and citrus between Africa, Arabia and Asia.
  • 15th–19th centuries: Crops like maize, cassava, tomatoes and chillies arrive from the Americas and reshape local cooking.
  • The diaspora: Enslaved Africans carry techniques and ingredients such as okra and rice across the Atlantic, shaping Southern US, Caribbean and Brazilian food.
  • Today: Jollof, suya, tagine and injera appear in restaurants worldwide, inspiring a new generation of chefs.

Find a food landmark

Visit the famous Jemaa el-Fnaa market square in Marrakech, where food stalls serve tagines, grilled meats and fresh orange juice every evening.
